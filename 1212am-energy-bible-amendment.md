## 18. Two harvest classes

A node is an **energy harvester**. That is what a zone is.

| Class | Where | What it harvests | Fiat till | £ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | store · Dragon12 · Golden Dragon | work, presence in the room, stock leaving the shelf, a paid order | **yes** — fiat already rings here | may issue £ against written energy, from that node’s limit |
| **Non-physical** | Wormhole | feeling — reacts, messages, presence in channel | **no** | may issue tiny £ from Wormhole’s limit only. **No path into fiat.** |

### Physical — energy meets fiat in the same room

Energy arrives (a shift, a session, a customer, a plate).  
Fiat already lives here (TWD / GBP / card).  
£nergy is the book that writes the energy and may issue £.

This is **not** “£ converts to fiat.”  
This is: **the same shelf can be rung in fiat and/or in £.**  
Fiat in at a tagged order may cause that physical node to issue £ from its own limit. That is issuance against a cash event, still not a booth.

### Wormhole — feeling, no fiat

Feeling is energy. It is written (micro, capped).  
It does not ring a till.  
It does not become TWD or GBP.  
That is the bridge: one book (`.£`) sees the room *and* the feeling. Only the room has fiat.

## 19. What “bridge” means here

£nergy does not turn feeling into cash.  
£nergy does not pay the wall.  
£nergy is the **one book** that can hold:

- physical harvest (store, Dragon12, Golden Dragon)  
- feeling harvest (Wormhole)  

and can let £ issued at a physical node clear at that node’s shelf — next to fiat.

Traditional models are **moulds**, not masters. We use a till because a till exists. We do not import the till’s story that only cash is real.

### `.energy` / `.daily` — same command

1. Type `.energy` (or `.daily`) in Discord.
2. The bot reads the clock **now**.
3. You get £.

| Clock | £ |
|---|---|
| 12⋮12 | 12 |
| 12⋮21 · 21⋮12 · 21⋮21 · 06⋮39 · 09⋮36 | 2 |
| anything else | 1 |

First tap of the day pays. Later tap only if the clock is better. Miss a day, fire dies. Fire 3 · 6 · 9 · 12 = treat +1 £.

The card: `+12 £` · FIRE · GIF · Bank. Then it vanishes.

# Play

Discord is the game. Bank is the book.

**Every member. No Discord role.**

| Type | Does |
|---|---|
| `.£` | Your pile |
| `.energy` | Play. Clock pays. Same as `.daily` |
| `.shop` | Buy. `$` or `£` as marked |
| `.receipt` | Last 8 |
| `.inventory` | What you own |

Slash for the pile is `/e`. Cards last 3 seconds. Bank → https://bank.1212.is

## Play

Type `.energy`. The clock now pays.

| Clock | £ |
|---|---|
| 12⋮12 | 12 |
| 12⋮21 · 21⋮12 · 21⋮21 · 06⋮39 · 09⋮36 | 2 |
| else | 1 |

First tap of the day pays. Later tap only if the clock is better. Miss a day, fire dies. Fire 3 · 6 · 9 · 12 = treat +1 £.

Talk and react also add £. Silent. Cap 12 each per day.

## Two books

`.£` is £nergy. `.$` is frozen WH. Shop items say which book they take. Old `$` still spends.

- The bot never holds or moves fiat.

## 2. What £nergy is

**Energy** is what happened. It is written when a **node can see it**.
**£** is an IOU that node writes, only from its **node limit**, only against written energy, only at its published rate.

Rule: **£ is never printed.** A node accepts energy and goes negative by the same £. It clears only when someone spends £ there and something real leaves the shelf.

- No central mint. Not the bot, not the bank, not the operator.
- Operator sets **world settings** (limits, rates, standing). Operator cannot type £ into an account.
- Players cannot go below zero. Only nodes carry obligation.
- No welcome £. No WH → £.
- **£ never replaces wages** for employed staff.
- Supply of £ = sum of node obligations. Always.

## 3. Measure is not issue

What a node can see is **written**.
What a node can honour may pay **£**.
If the node is at its limit, the row still writes energy; £ = 0; reason = limit.

Unseen is not “zero energy.” It is **unrowed**.

**Written when visible**

- Shift / task / session at a physical node, witnessed or machine
- QR visit in a room
- Tagged paid order at the store
- Staff `.issue` (standing + that node’s limit)
- Documented file — pending until approved, then full; 0 £ until approved
- Wormhole feeling (reacts, messages, channel presence) — **micro**, daily cap, diminishing; after the cap store a **tally**, do not insert dead rows

**Never written**

- Thoughts
- Private chat, other apps, street gossip, family tables
- Follower graphs, mutuals, who is behind an account

Energy exists off the map. The book does not follow it.

## 4. The loop (player copy — these words only)

You do a thing in a place we can see.
We write it under `.£`.
If that place can honour it, it issues £ and goes into debt.
You spend £. Something leaves the shelf. The debt shrinks.

When the **node limit** is full: we still write the work. We pay 0 £ until people spend there.
You do not raise the limit because the gap looks bad. That is the old WH printer.
Limit rises only with real clearing or real new capacity, operator, 7 days’ notice.

90 days uncleared: node **frozen**. It can accept £. It cannot issue.

## 5. The wall

Fiat still pays what is **not a node**: landlord, power, tax, wholesale, employed wages, strangers.

**£ never pays the wall.**
No cash-out. No node holds a TWD or GBP reserve. No `1 £ = N TWD`.
Crypto used as payment is a **pipe** into a real SKU at a physical node. It is not a third book and not a mint.

### Fiat shrinks only two ways (monthly rates, no date)

1. Share of **inside** exchange that settled in £ rather than fiat.
2. Fiat no longer sent over the wall because a member node now makes the thing (**import substitution**, L1).

£ does not reduce what the wall demands.
£ reduces fiat burned on internal labour and goods, so more incoming fiat can stay on rent and stock.

Do not write: TWD ends · fiat obsolete · flywheel that does not leak · absorb all money · energy converts to fiat.
Write: **less leak**, measured.
At a physical node, energy **meets** fiat. It does not convert into fiat.

## 6. Two tills, no booth

A SKU at a physical node may be: **fiat-only** · **dual** · **£-preferred** · **£-only**.
Change is per SKU, per node, 7 days’ notice. No absorption day.

Fiat buys the shelf.
£ is the promise.
They meet at the till.
They do not trade with each other.

`.give` moves £ between players (zero-sum, no new energy). A balance changes hands. That is not exchange for fiat.

Wormhole has no till. Feeling never rings fiat.

## 8. Tier (L2 policy)

TEAM 1212: **SURVIVOR / OWN IDENTITY / SUPER POWER**.

- SURVIVOR — streak, any written energy.
- OWN IDENTITY — first **macro** energy at `witnessed` or `machine` at a physical node. Opens L1 exits **as policy**, not as physics.
- SUPER POWER — macro energy at ≥ 2 physical nodes in 90 days, and brought someone to OWN IDENTITY.

Tier reads **macro + witnessed/machine**. Wormhole feeling cannot climb OWN IDENTITY or SUPER POWER.
Balance in £ does not decay. Tier score does.

## 9. Echoes and masks

Copying a crest / drop / phrase = L2. Taking stock = L1.

- Public mark, no delivery → library `kind: echo` (url, which mark, date). No hunt. No £.
- Public mark **and** a delivered outcome → committed energy. Operator may **invite** them as a node or player. They keep their name unless they take the crest.
- Do not write “acquire all identical energy.” Scale + a better shelf.
- Selling the mark = operator legal task, not a bot command.
- No unmask. No scrape.

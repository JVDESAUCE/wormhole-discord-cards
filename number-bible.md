# 12⋮12am — one tap

**Updated:** 2026-09-19 · **By:** grok  
**Rule:** `.energy` and `.daily` are the **same play**. One tap pays. Time uses **⋮**.

## Play

Hit `.energy` or `.daily`. Same function. The clock you are on decides £.

| Clock | £ |
|---|---|
| 12⋮12 | 12 |
| 12⋮21 · 21⋮12 · 21⋮21 · 06⋮39 · 09⋮36 | 2 |
| anything else | 1 |

`09⋮63` is **not a clock**. Use 06⋮39.

First tap of the day pays. Later taps only pay if the clock is better (the difference). Hunt 12⋮12.

## Fire

Days in a row you played. Skip a day → 0.

On fire **3 · 6 · 9 · 12**, add **+1 £**. Not multiplied.

Fire 12 + 12⋮12 = 12 + 1 = **13 £**. Not 144.

## Commands

`.energy` = `.daily` = play.  
`.£` = the pile. Slash `/e`.  
`.shop` = buy.  
`.receipt` = last 8.  
`.$` = frozen WH. Different book.

**Every member can type** `.£` `.daily` `.energy` `.shop` `.receipt`. No Discord role.
